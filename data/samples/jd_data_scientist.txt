Junior Data Scientist

About the role
We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist to build and ship machine learning products.

Requirements
- Strong Python and SQL skills.
- Hands-on experience with Pandas and Scikit-learn.
- Experience building REST APIs with FastAPI or Flask.
- Familiarity with Docker and Git.
- Experience with Spark for large-scale data processing.
- Working knowledge of AWS.
- Experience with A/B testing and statistics.

Nice to have
- Experience with LLM evaluation or RAG systems.
- Tableau or Power BI.
